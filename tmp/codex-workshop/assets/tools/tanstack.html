Principles

Why TanStack?

Our libraries are built around real products and the developers shipping them.

  1. Portable core

    Framework Agnostic

    Our library cores are provider-agnostic and logic-driven, meaning you can use the same logic in React, Vue, Svelte, Solid, and more.

    ReactVueSolidAngularVanillacore

  2. Compile-time contracts

    Type-Safe by Design

    Built with TypeScript from the ground up, providing incredible autocomplete and safety across your entire data-fetching and state management stack.

    params.postIdstring
    query.dataProject[]
    form.emailField<string>

  3. Real workloads

    Production-Grade

    Battle-tested in the world's largest apps. We build for scale, handling complex concurrency, caching, and state synchronization with ease.

  4. Independent tools

    No Vendor Lock-in

    Open source and independent. We aren't beholden to any single cloud provider or framework team, ensuring the best tools for the community.
