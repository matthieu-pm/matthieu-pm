# Above-the-fold recruiter audit

## Scope

- Surface: desktop portfolio above the fold
- Audience: startup recruiter hiring a Product Manager or Design Engineer
- Evidence: `01-above-the-fold.jpg` plus the user-provided fold screenshot

## Rating

- Product Manager fit: 8/10
- Design Engineer fit: 7/10
- Overall first-pass impression: 7.5/10
- Recruiter outcome: continue reading

## Strengths

- Calm, polished visual design signals craft and restraint.
- The founding product engineer role at Forge is a strong startup credential.
- Two shipped projects appear early and make the profile feel concrete.
- Education, growth experience, AI, UX, code, and contact paths are visible without navigation.
- Internship timing is explicit.

## Risks

- The headline positions the candidate as “Product Manager · Growth & UX,” while the target role also includes Design Engineer.
- The strongest differentiator—combining product judgment, design craft, and implementation—is distributed across several paragraphs instead of stated immediately.
- Project cards explain what the products are, but not the candidate’s role, ownership, or result.
- Contact links are available, but the primary recruiter action is not visually prioritized.
- The long introduction requires more reading than a typical first-pass recruiter scan.

## Highest-impact recommendations

1. Align the headline with the target: make Product Manager and Design Engineer explicit.
2. Lead with a one-sentence value proposition focused on shipping products from insight to implementation.
3. Add a compact ownership or proof line to each project card.
4. Pull availability and résumé access into a more scannable recruiter action.

## Accessibility limits

- The screenshot shows a logical visual reading order and readable contrast.
- Keyboard behavior, focus order, hover-only information, zoom reflow, and screen-reader output require implementation testing and cannot be confirmed from the screenshot alone.
