## Content Signals

These values record the policy already declared in `/robots.txt`, `/llms.txt`,
`/llms-full.txt`, and `/llms.md`:

```text
Content-Signal: search=yes, ai-input=yes, ai-train=yes
```

- Search: yes.
- AI input: yes.
- AI training: yes.

The shared implementation value is in `apps/web/lib/content-policy.ts`.
`/robots.txt` is generated by `apps/web/app/robots.txt/route.ts`.

additional features that are absent from the source files.
